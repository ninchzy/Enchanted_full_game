ENCHANTED
=========

1. Unzip this whole folder somewhere (don't run it from inside the zip).
2. Double-click Play.bat.
   A black window opens and the game starts in your web browser.
   Keep the black window open while you play; close it when you're done.

   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Controls
  W A S D ........ walk
  Shift .......... run (careful: he can hear you)
  SPACE .......... look at things, talk, confirm
  TAB ............ inventory
  ESC ............ menu / back

Your saves are kept in the browser, so play in the same browser each time.
Turn your sound on.
